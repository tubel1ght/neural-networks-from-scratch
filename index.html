Neural Networks from Scratch

An interactive course that builds a neural network one honest step at a time — starting from a single robot shot, ending at backpropagation. Move the sliders. Watch the model respond. No math background required.

One story, all the way through

Every lesson models the same imaginary "reality": how likely a robot's shot is to go in, based on three things you could read off a photo of the shot — its arc (x1), its closeness to the goal (x2), and how clear the shot is (x3). You'll start by exploring that reality, then slowly learn to rebuild it yourself out of simple bendable pieces — which turns out to be exactly what a neural network does.

make % = 100 · exp((x1 − 50 − 0.3·x2)² / −3000) · ln((x2 + 10) / 10) / ln(11) · (x3 / 100)

You never have to understand this formula. It's just the hidden "truth" the model is trying to learn — the same truth in every lesson. (x1 = shot arc, x2 = closeness to goal, x3 = shot clearness.)
