Reality equation

This hidden "truth" turns three shot features into a make-probability (0–100%). You can edit it, but you never need to understand it.

See the −0.3·x2 tucked inside the arc part? That's the one place the features interact: the ideal arc shifts a little as the shot gets closer to the goal (closer shots want a slightly higher arc). It's why the make-probability "sweet spot" drifts as you change closeness.
